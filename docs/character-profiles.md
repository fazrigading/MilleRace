# Character Profiles & Lore Specifications 📜

> Original character quotes, dialogue lore, reading recommendations, and misinformation toolkits for **Miller**, **Jen**, **Aidan**, and **Lizzy** in **MilleRace**.

## 🦊 Miller

```
Miller loves finding clues for his adventures! 
But he often gets lost without his keys.
That is why thinking critically is important!
```

### Misinformation & Critical Thinking Resources
1. [University of Sheffield — Approaches to Thinking Critically](https://sheffield.ac.uk/study-skills/research/approaches/thinking-critically)
2. [Bookr Class — Critical Thinking Activities for Kids](https://bookrclass.com/blog/critical-thinking-activities-for-kids/)

### Final Result Profile & Recommendations (Score: 1–25 Points)
* **Proficiency Rating:** PISA Reading Level 1–2 | Cambridge Reading A1–A2
* **Persona:** Curious and ready for new chapters! Miller has a long way ahead to go. He’d love to read more, one page at a time. Comic books suit his personality, and so do fun facts!
* **Recommended Activities:** Reading nighttime bedtime stories and doing crosswords.
* **Curated Book Recommendations:**
  1. *Charlotte’s Web* by E. B. White ([Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/CharlottesWeb))
  2. *Keluarga Cemara* by Arswendo Atmowiloto ([Perpustakaan Nasional Digital](https://kios-perpustakaan.jakarta.go.id/catalogue/detail/99218))

## ⚡ Jen

```
Jen knows everything!
But she never really knows what to trust.
She always checks… factually!
```

### Misinformation Defense & Fact-Checking Resources
1. [Reuters Fact Check](https://www.reuters.com/fact-check/)
2. [MediaSmarts — Break the Fake Campaign](https://mediasmarts.ca/break-fake)

### Final Result Profile & Recommendations (Score: 26–50 Points)
* **Proficiency Rating:** PISA Reading Level 3–4 | Cambridge Reading B1
* **Persona:** Witty and energetic, Jen is exploring the world! She has imaginary friends and places to be! Short stories and fairy tales are where she goes! She is inspired by real-life stories and writing in her diary!
* **Recommended Activities:** Daily journaling, writing in personal diary, comparing news stories.
* **Curated Book Recommendations:**
  1. *The Fault in Our Stars* by John Green ([Google Books](https://books.google.co.id/books/about/The_Fault_in_Our_Stars.html?hl=id&id=Qk8n0olOX5MC&redir_esc=y))
  2. *Laskar Pelangi* by Andrea Hirata (Available in regional public libraries)

## 🦉 Aidan

```
Aidan loves browsing the internet!
But he only accepts legit sources.
He sees citations and timelines!
```

### Citation Verification & Research Resources
1. [Penn State University Libraries — Citation Searching & Bibliometrics](https://guides.libraries.psu.edu/bibliometrics/citationsearching)
2. [Academic References & Citations in Academic Writing](https://medium.com/@kichu.josef/all-you-need-to-know-about-references-citations-in-academic-writing-9174922d5f9e)

### Final Result Profile & Recommendations (Score: 51–75 Points)
* **Proficiency Rating:** PISA Reading Level 5 | Cambridge Reading B2
* **Persona:** Adventurous and resilient, Aidan is on the move! Books are his window to the world. He reads articles about his favorite characters, loves reading series, and watching films adapted from them!
* **Recommended Activities:** Reading multi-volume series, inspecting primary source citations, tracking historical timelines.
* **Curated Book Recommendations:**
  1. *1984* by George Orwell ([Internet Archive PDF](https://dn790002.ca.archive.org/0/items/NineteenEightyFour-Novel-GeorgeOrwell/orwell1984.pdf))
  2. *Gadis Kretek* by Ratih Kumala (Available at Bank Indonesia Library & regional archives)

## 🎨 Lizzy

```
Lizzy’s lies set on lies!
She reads graphs like the Egyptian pyramid.
Statistics got nothing on her!
She makes art works from what she reads!
```

### Data Literacy & Graphic Analysis Resources
1. [UC Berkeley School of Information — Beginner's Guide to Data Literacy](https://ischoolonline.berkeley.edu/blog/beginners-guide-improving-data-literacy/)
2. [Dataversity — Data Literacy Essentials](https://www.dataversity.net/articles/data-literacy-essentials-what-you-need-to-know/)

### Final Result Profile & Recommendations (Score: 76–100 Points)
* **Proficiency Rating:** PISA Reading Level 6 | Cambridge Reading C1
* **Persona:** Lizzy is the wiz! She reads like there’s no tomorrow. She’s always all guarded! The library and bookstores need her. She makes artworks from what she reads!
* **Recommended Activities:** Creating literature-inspired artwork, data visualization critique, leading community reading discussions.
* **Curated Book Recommendations:**
  1. *The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde* by Robert Louis Stevenson ([Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/43/43-h/43-h.htm))
  2. *Bumi Manusia* by Pramoedya Ananta Toer (Available in local public libraries)
