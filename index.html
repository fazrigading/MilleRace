Decoding the AI Horizon with MilleRace

A gamified relay race developed to address the Indonesian literacy paradox, train critical reading, and cultivate discernment in an era of generative AI.

📖 The Indonesian Literacy Paradox

Data from Badan Pusat Statistik (2024) indicates that only 14 out of 34 provinces in Indonesia have established public libraries with accessible catalog data. With average library borrowing of just 2 items per year and low triennial PISA scores, access has historically been a bottleneck.

Yet, seasonal book exhibitions such as Semesta Buku and Big Bad Wolf witness over 5 million new books purchased annually. The curiosity exists—what readers need is an accessible, engaging, and modern entry point.

🤖 The Rise of Generative AI

As AI tools synthesize realistic art and hallucinated articles in seconds, distinguishing human intentionality from algorithmic output has become a foundational 21st-century survival skill.

MilleRace translates academic media literacy frameworks into an immersive 3-minute challenge that encourages analytical thinking without punitive grades.

The Artificial Intelligence Assessment Scale (AIAS)

⚖️
Symmetry
Evaluating synthetic geometric perfection versus human balance and natural asymmetry.
🎭
Expressionalism
Measuring genuine emotional nuance versus superficial prompt replication.
🔍
Distinctions
Identifying unique human stylistic strokes and quirks vs repetitive model artifacts.
📐
Proportionality
Spotting anatomical anomalies, unnatural tangents, and spatial logic errors in artwork.
💡
Memorability
Distinguishing original conceptual storytelling from generic vocabulary templates.

Vision & Strategic Plan

Empowering Readers in a
Synthetic Age

Our mission is to transform standardized literacy evaluation into a delightful, empowering journey that connects readers with real public libraries and digital repositories.

5 Strategic Pillars

01
PILLAR 01 CORE FRAMEWORK
⏱️ 3-Min Relay Maze

Gamify Standardized MIL Testing

Transforming complex PISA, Cambridge, and TOEFL literacy frameworks into an intuitive, non-intimidating 3-minute timed relay maze. Players navigate adaptive challenges that test speed, analytical deduction, and deep contextual comprehension without diagnostic fatigue.

🎯
PISA & Cambridge Aligned Calibrated against international benchmark competencies.
⚡
4-Room Timed Circuit Rapid-fire puzzles measuring cognitive speed & recall.
🎮
Zero Intimidation Gamified escape mechanics that make assessment engaging.
📊
Granular Analytics Live skill radar tracking comprehension across 4 dimensions.
💡
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Reduces diagnostic anxiety while providing educators and learners with objective, actionable literacy metrics within 180 seconds.
02
PILLAR 02 AI DEFENSE & SAFETY
🤖 AIAS Scale Mapped

Raise AI Literacy & Awareness

Utilizing the Artificial Intelligence Assessment Scale (AIAS) to train readers in spotting synthetic imagery, repetitive prompt patterns, and generative AI hallucinations in contemporary media feeds.

🔬
AIAS Classification Tiered framework for evaluating machine-generated writing.
👁️
Synthetic Artifact Auditing Detecting visual anomalies, unnatural textures & fake quotes.
🧠
Hallucination Defense Identifying plausible-sounding yet false AI claims.
🛡️
Prompt Engineering Literacy Demystifying how LLMs construct synthetic narratives.
💡
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Inoculates youth against automated disinformation campaigns and deceptive synthetic generative content.
03
PILLAR 03 PUBLIC ARCHIVE INTEGRATION
🏛️ Perpusnas & Open Repos

Bridge Local & Digital Libraries

Directly connecting game outcomes to Indonesia's premier public library networks (Perpustakaan Nasional Digital, Bank Indonesia Library) and global open repositories (Project Gutenberg, Internet Archive).

📖
Perpusnas Digital Link Seamless onboarding to national Indonesian eBook archives.
🏦
Bank Indonesia Repositories Access to economic, civic, and historical literary assets.
🌐
Open Knowledge Repositories Direct links to 70,000+ public-domain Project Gutenberg titles.
📍
Samarinda City Outreach School-to-library roadshows and youth literacy workshops.
💡
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Converts momentary gameplay excitement into enduring, tangible engagement with public educational institutions.
04
PILLAR 04 HUMAN-CENTRIC GROWTH
🎭 4 Supportive Archetypes

Promote Graded Reading Without Stigma

Matching reader proficiencies to supportive character archetypes (Miller, Jen, Aidan, Lizzy) with personalized reading roadmaps, encouraging progress rather than punitive scoring.

🎭
Archetype Companions Miller (Discovery), Jen (Inquiry), Aidan (Analysis), Lizzy (Mastery).
🌱
Stigma-Free Feedback Growth-oriented language celebrating curiosity at every stage.
📚
Custom Book Prescriptions Curated reading pathways adapted to each player's archetype.
📈
Progressive Stepping Stones Clear pathways for ascending from early reader to master analyst.
💡
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Builds lifelong reading confidence and intrinsic motivation across all demographic tiers.
05
PILLAR 05 FACTUAL INTEGRITY
🛡️ Veracity Shield

Mitigate Digital Misinformation

Instilling reflexive verification habits: citation cross-checking, timeline reconstruction, author credential audit, and graphical distortion detection in fast-paced media feeds.

🔎
Primary Citation Tracing Techniques to unmask fake citations and circular references.
⚖️
Lateral Reading Habits Cross-verifying claims across independent reliable sources.
📊
Data & Chart Deconstruction Identifying truncated axes, misleading scales, and cherry-picked data.
⏱️
Timeline & Context Auditing Detecting out-of-context archival imagery recirculated as breaking news.
💡
STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Creates a generation of resilient, critical-thinking digital citizens capable of navigating complex information landscapes.
